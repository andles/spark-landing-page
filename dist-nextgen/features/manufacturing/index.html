Manufacturing

Transform raw materials into finished products with complete visibility and control over your production process.

Key Features

Bills of Materials (BOM)

Define multi-level product recipes with components, sub-assemblies, and raw materials. Support for phantom BOMs and variants.

Manufacturing Orders

Plan and schedule production runs based on demand. Track progress from raw materials to finished goods.

Work Order Management

Create and manage work orders with detailed operation sequences. Track labor, materials, and machine time.

Operation Tracking & Scheduling

Define manufacturing operations with time estimates and dependencies. Schedule work across available resources.

Machine Management

Track machine availability, maintenance schedules, and utilization. Assign operations to specific machines.

Production Materials Tracking

Monitor raw material consumption and availability. Automatic material requisitions based on production schedules.

Shop Floor Control

Real-time visibility into production status. Workers can clock in/out of operations and report completions.

Work Order Approval Workflows

Configurable approval processes for work orders. Ensure proper authorization before production begins.

Yield & Scrap Tracking

Track actual vs expected output. Record scrap and waste with reason codes for continuous improvement.
