Core Feature

Inventory

Complete control over your product catalog and stock levels. Track every item across all locations with real-time accuracy.

Key Features

Items & SKU Management

Create and manage products with unique SKUs, multiple variants, pricing tiers, and detailed specifications. Support for serialized and lot-tracked items.

Bundle Items & Kits

Group multiple products together as bundles or kits. Automatically track component inventory when bundles are sold.

Item Groups & Categories

Organize your catalog with hierarchical categories and groups. Apply bulk settings and pricing rules to entire groups.

Custom Fields

Extend item records with unlimited custom fields. Track any data specific to your business needs.

Barcode Generation & Printing

Generate and print barcodes and labels for all your items. Support for multiple barcode formats including UPC, EAN, and QR codes.

Real-Time Stock Levels

View accurate, real-time inventory quantities across all locations. Set reorder points and receive low stock alerts.

Inventory Adjustments

Make quick adjustments for damaged goods, cycle counts, or corrections. Full audit trail for all changes.

Write-Offs & Shrinkage

Track and manage inventory write-offs with reason codes. Generate reports on shrinkage and loss.

Item History & Audit Trail

Complete visibility into every transaction affecting an item. Track who changed what and when.
