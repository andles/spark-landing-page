Where the decision gets hard

Five tradeoffs worth deciding explicitly

These are evaluation themes—not anonymous customer quotations. The factual details are checked against the vendor’s published pricing and product material.

Growth meter01

Move when the order cap becomes the plan

Zoho’s limits are transparent and rational for smaller operations. They become friction when the team is managing order volume and add-on blocks instead of choosing features.

Zoho Inventory

Published tiers range from 50 to 15,000 orders per month, with additional order blocks available.

Spark

Spark does not publish an order-volume ceiling; pricing scales by users, warehouses, capabilities, and AI volume.

Planning depth02

Go beyond the reorder threshold

Zoho covers reorder levels and replenishment. Spark is designed to combine demand, stock, incoming supply, and lead times into a draft purchasing decision.

Zoho Inventory

Zoho provides a broad SMB inventory and order-management toolkit at a low price.

Spark

Spark concentrates more of the product around forecast-to-approval inventory planning.

System of record03

Keep QuickBooks and commerce at the center

Zoho Inventory is at its most cohesive alongside Zoho Books, CRM, and Commerce. Spark is designed as the inventory layer around Shopify, Amazon, and QuickBooks Online.

Zoho Inventory

The Zoho suite is a real advantage when the rest of the business already lives there.

Spark

Spark does not ask the team to move its commerce or accounting stack into a broader suite.

Agent workflow04

Operate through Sparki or your own assistant

Spark’s MCP tools expose governed onboarding and operational workflows to compatible AI assistants, with previews and approval gates before writes.

Zoho Inventory

Zoho offers APIs, webhooks, custom functions, and a large automation ecosystem.

Spark

Spark makes agent-led inventory work a first-class product path instead of a custom integration project.

Price honesty05

Zoho wins the entry-price comparison

A credible comparison should say this plainly: Spark is not the cheaper stock ledger. It earns consideration only when planning depth or operating friction is worth the difference.

Zoho Inventory

Zoho has a forever-free plan and paid plans starting at $29 per organization per month when billed annually.

Spark

Spark starts at $199 per month and is positioned as an inventory planning and execution system.

The fit check

When to stay. When to look at Spark.

Zoho Inventory is a very strong value when the operation fits its caps and suite. Spark becomes relevant when the planning gap costs more than the software price difference.

Stay on Zoho Inventory when

  • You are under the order cap and the price-to-capability ratio is working.
  • Zoho Books, CRM, or Commerce is already the center of the business.
  • In-app shipping labels and strong locations-per-dollar matter most.

Look at Spark when

  • The order meter is driving plan changes more than the capabilities are.
  • QuickBooks Online—not Zoho Books—is the accounting system of record.
  • You want the system to forecast, draft the buy, and wait for approval.

Zoho Inventory migration FAQ

Questions buyers ask before switching

How does Spark migrate data out of Zoho Inventory?+

Bring your Zoho Inventory exports to Sparki in app, or use a compatible AI assistant through Spark MCP. Spark profiles the files, maps and validates the records, and presents the proposed import for approval.

Does Spark replace Zoho Inventory or Zoho Books?+

Spark replaces the inventory and purchasing layer, not Zoho Books, CRM, or Commerce. Spark’s native accounting workflow is built around QuickBooks Online.

Why consider Spark when Zoho Inventory starts at $29?+

Do not switch for price. Consider Spark when order caps, a non-Zoho accounting stack, or the work required to build each purchase plan has become the larger operating cost.

Does Spark connect to Shopify, Amazon, and QuickBooks Online?+

Yes. Spark is designed around Shopify, Amazon, and QuickBooks Online inventory workflows, while leaving Shopify as the commerce platform.

Can we evaluate Spark before moving everything?+

Yes. Start free or book a working session using your own Zoho Inventory exports, then decide whether the planning workflow is worth a broader migration.
