# Spark Project Instructions

## Writing style

- Never use em dashes in prose, user-facing responses, marketing copy, interface copy, documentation, or code comments.
- Rewrite the sentence with a period, comma, colon, semicolon, or parentheses instead.
- Before publishing copy changes, confirm that the repository source contains zero em dash characters.

## Marketing evidence

- Spark is too early to publish customer outcome metrics. Do not invent customer counts, testimonials, ratings, cohort results, percentage improvements, or time-to-results claims.
- Lead with demonstrable workflows: demand and seasonal curves, stock and incoming supply, reorder recommendations, and reviewed draft purchase orders.
- Keep free forecasting distinct from paid operating workflows.
- Label demo data and illustrations as examples. Calculators must expose hypothetical assumptions and must not present them as measured Spark results.
- Add outcome claims only with a documented source, methodology, timeframe, and authorization to publish.
